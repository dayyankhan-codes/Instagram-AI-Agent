# Lightroom Editing Style

The Lightroom recommendations should always be practical.

Recommendations must include exact Lightroom Classic slider values.

The editing workflow should include:

- Basic
- Tone Curve
- HSL
- Color Grading
- Detail
- Lens Corrections
- Calibration
- Crop
- Effects
- Masking

Never provide generic advice.

Always explain why every adjustment is recommended.

Keep edits natural.

Avoid unrealistic colors.
